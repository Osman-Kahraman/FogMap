# Privacy Policy for FogMap

Effective Date: May 2026

FogMap respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## Authentication

FogMap provides secure authentication using:

- Email & Password authentication  
- Sign in with Apple  
- Google Sign-In  

Authentication is securely handled through trusted third-party providers. FogMap does not store your Apple, Google, or external account passwords.

## Data Collection

FogMap may collect and store limited user information necessary to provide core app functionality, including:

- Email address  
- First name / last name (if provided)  
- Nationality (if provided)  
- Visited countries  
- Basic account information  

This information is stored securely using Firebase services.

## Location Data

FogMap uses your device’s location to power its core exploration experience.

Location access is used to:

- Reveal explored areas on the map  
- Track exploration progress  
- Detect visited countries  
- Provide location-based app functionality  

FogMap only accesses location data with your permission.

## Data Usage

Collected data is used solely to:

- Provide account functionality  
- Display user exploration progress and statistics  
- Maintain personalized exploration history  
- Improve app performance and user experience  

FogMap does not sell or monetize personal user data.

## Third-Party Services

FogMap relies on trusted third-party services, including:

- Firebase Authentication  
- Cloud Firestore (Firebase)  
- Google Sign-In  
- Sign in with Apple  
- Apple CloudKit (if enabled)

Please refer to their privacy policies for additional information:

- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy  
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Sharing

FogMap does not sell, rent, or share personal user information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may only be processed through required infrastructure providers used to operate the app.

## Data Security

Reasonable security measures are implemented to help protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
